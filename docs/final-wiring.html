MACH32 RTOS / HARDWARE REFERENCE

Final motor-controller wiring

NUCLEO-F446RE · L293D · potentiometer · HW-131 · DC motor

Disconnect STM32 USB and the battery before changing wiring. This is the final connection plan used during bench bring-up, not an electrical certification. Motor stall current and regulator output were not measured. Never connect the 9 V battery directly to the motor. PWM duty limiting is not current protection.

04 / Knob and motor power module

Knob: three terminals, no polarity markings needed

Middle terminal → A0 / PA0 / CN8 pin 1. One outside terminal → 3V3 / CN6 pin 4. Other outside terminal → GND (CN6 pin 7 is a separate available ground socket). Any other existing STM32 GND connection is electrically equivalent; do not put two jumper pins in one socket.

Swapping the outside terminals reverses the knob direction. Never feed 5V into this ADC connection.

HW-131: use the fixed output header, not the breadboard rail contacts

Orient this module with the barrel jack at upper right and USB-A socket below it, matching the assembly session. Read its printed labels; variants can differ.

Central 8-pin header, top view:

         3.3V   3.3V    5V     5V ← to J49 (L293D pin 8)
         GND    GND     GND    GND ← to D52 (common ground)

The two highlighted connections are the rightmost vertical pair. They are separate output pins: do NOT bridge them with a jumper cap. Leave the rail-selector jumpers unchanged; their underside rail contacts are unused. Keep the module separate from the breadboard and its underside pins clear of metal.

9V battery → snap-to-barrel lead → DC-in jack. The module's fixed regulated 5V output supplies the driver motor input. Its USB-A socket is unused. Use the battery snap as an accessible physical disconnect.

The Nucleo 5V and external module 5V must NOT be joined. Nucleo 5V supplies L293D pin 16 only; external 5V supplies pin 8 only. Their grounds ARE joined. Neither motor terminal connects directly to a power rail.

05 / Operation and limits

  • Firmware defaults to motor output disabled and a 25% maximum duty. A separate 50%-limit motion-enabled build was used during bring-up. Wiring does not determine which build is flashed: check STATUS fields outputs and limit.
  • ARM POT enables continuous forward-only knob control. It continues without a host heartbeat; closing a dashboard does not stop it. STOP requests an output stop; disconnect motor power for a physical stop.
  • Knob position controls PWM demand, not measured RPM. No encoder or current sensor is present. If the motor only buzzes without rotating, remove motor power; do not leave it stalled.
  • Secure the motor, remove any exposed fan blade, keep its shaft clear, and disconnect if the driver, regulator, battery or motor heats abnormally. Do not leave the bench running unattended.

Basic motor movement and input/firmware bring-up were demonstrated. This diagram does not establish sustained-load reliability, stall-current compatibility, measured regulator voltage, or worst-case timing. The 9V/regulator arrangement follows the kit tutorial, not a universal recommendation for unidentified motors.
